Every tap moves the worm.

Numbered tape marks show the woodworm's next three steps. If it lands on a free wing nut, it knocks it out, into its tin or onto your bench. If not, it bores a hole in the top flat, and sometimes that hole shows a nut hidden underneath.

So every tap is two moves: yours, and the worm's. Plan around it, or use it.

How it plays

  1. Tap a free wing nut and it flies to the open tin of its colour. Each tin holds three, two tins are open at a time, and you can see the next one.
  2. No matching tin? The nut waits on a five-place bench. Fill the bench and the scene ends.
  3. When its last nut comes out, a flat falls. Clear the stage and the curtain comes down.
  4. 60 scenes in three acts. The first scenes each teach one rule, and How to play is always one tap away.

Three tools in the wings

Top Hat

One more bench place. It also rescues a full bench.

Spanner

Take any nut, even one that is covered.

Intermission

The woodworm rests for five taps.

You start with two of each and earn more on every fifth scene and at the end of each act.

Made by hand

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. All 60 scenes are free to play. The Stagehand Kit and the Spotlight are optional.

Does it need the internet?

No. Propworm plays fully offline.

I reinstalled the game. How do I get the Spotlight back?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same App Store account.

Which devices?

iPhone, from the App Store. It is not out yet; email me and I will tell you when it is.

Does it collect data?

No. Nothing leaves your device except purchases, which go through the App Store. See the privacy policy.
