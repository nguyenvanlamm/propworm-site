How to play

Tap a free wing nut and it flies to the open tin of its colour; each tin holds three. With no matching tin open, the nut waits on the bench, and a full bench ends the scene. Every tap, the woodworm takes one step along its numbered marks: it knocks out a free nut it lands on, or bores a hole in the top flat. A flat falls when its last nut is out. The game also has a How to play page in Settings and in the Pause menu.

Purchases

All 60 scenes are free. The Stagehand Kit adds five of each tool and can be bought again; the Spotlight is a one-time unlock. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore Purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
