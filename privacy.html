Privacy Policy

Propworm · last updated 12 November 2026

Propworm is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the highest scene you have cleared and how many Top Hats, Spanners and Intermissions you have.
  • Your settings: whether sound is on.
  • Whether you have bought the Spotlight, and the store's transaction ids for purchases already delivered, so a purchase is never granted twice.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

All 60 scenes are free. The Stagehand Kit and the Spotlight are optional purchases, handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
